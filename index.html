Sources & limitations

Nomix: Fabio distribution in Italy supplies approximate counts for all 20 regions. Their sum is 160,957, matching the national total on the source page. The page does not state the counts’ reference date or estimation method. These are published estimates, not official registration counts.

ISTAT: demographic indicators, 2024 supplies the population denominator: Table 1 of the statistical tables, total resident population at 1 January 2025, marked provisional. Figures are published in thousands to one decimal place, then multiplied by 1,000 here; they are rounded to 100 residents.

Retrieved 11 October 2026. Regional prevalence divides the Nomix estimate by the ISTAT population; the two sources cannot be confirmed to describe the same date or population scope. The percentages are approximate comparisons. National distribution uses only the Nomix regional estimates, divided by their sum. Neither view measures newborn-name registrations. Nomix’s own regional detail pages can use differently rounded estimates; this map consistently uses its national table.

Population means all residents, of all sexes and ages. Use counts for the same date and population scope in every region. National share uses the sum of Fabio counts across all 20 regions; regional prevalence uses that region’s total population. Counts describe residents named Fabio, not newborn registrations.

Optional columns: source,as_of (repeat the same values on each row). Counts must be nonnegative integers; population must be positive and at least the Fabio count.
